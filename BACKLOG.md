# Backlog

The following ideas are **NOT IN CURRENT BUILD**. They are recorded for future evaluation only; do not implement them without an approved requirement and roadmap update.

## Task and platform ideas

- Recurring tasks
- Notifications and reminders
- Calendar integration or a complex calendar UI
- Lock-screen shortcut
- Native widgets
- Projects, labels, priorities, subtasks, or collaboration
- Task-manager integrations
- Persistent voice history
- Languages beyond the Sarvam Saaras v3 supported set, or language-specific UX beyond source-language transcription

## Business and expansion ideas

- Monetization, subscriptions, or payments
- Team workspaces
- Productivity scores or habits
- AI chatbot, meeting recording, or knowledge base

## Decision rule

Before promoting an item, verify that it reinforces Crisp’s voice-first capture promise, does not weaken the active-session safety boundary, and can be sequenced after the local voice experience works.
